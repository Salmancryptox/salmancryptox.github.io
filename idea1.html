THE TEN

Evolution. Time. Humanity's past and future.

About THE TEN

A sci-fi story that connects the origins of humanity with its possible future, exploring evolution, technology, and the consequences of moving through time.

At the center of the story are mysterious time machines that allow the boundaries between past, present, and future to collide.

As humanity discovers the ability to move through time, questions about evolution, technology, identity and the future of our species begin to emerge.

The Concept

What happens when humanity can look into its own past and its possible future?

THE TEN explores that question through a world where advanced technology makes time travel possible and humanity's history is no longer simply something to study. It becomes something that can be experienced.
